what changed: every solid was tessellated, the parts you can't see from outside
(the electronics, screws, clips and brackets, and the inner walls of what's
left) were dropped, and the rest was simplified and compressed into this one
glb. tools/frame_model.py in the framecorder repo is the script that does it.
